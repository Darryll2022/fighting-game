NEW MECHANICS
💨
DASH
Double-tap a direction to burst forward. Backdash grants brief invincibility frames — use it to escape pressure.
🦵
CROUCH ATTACK
Hold crouch then press Heavy to throw a low attack. Hits opponents who expect standing combos.
🌀
JUMP ATTACK
Jump and press Light in the air for an aerial strike. Harder to block — use it to open up defensive opponents.
💥
HIT STOP
Every landed hit freezes both fighters for a few frames. Heavy hits freeze longer — that pause is intentional.
🔥
COMBO COUNTER
Land hits within 2 seconds to build a combo chain. Counter appears above the attacker. Resets on timeout.
🛡️
PUSH-APART
Fighters can't overlap — they push each other. Use this to corner your opponent against the wall.

TIPS

  • Heavy ★ deals 25 dmg, Light deals 20
  • Backdash iframes beat meaty attacks
  • Corner pressure is real — use push-apart
  • Jump attacks beat grounded heavies
  • Crouch attacks punish high-attack spammers
  • Timer runs out → lower HP wins
  • Gold particles = heavy hit landed
  • Landing from jump kicks up dust
